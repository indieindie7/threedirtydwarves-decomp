Game: side-scrolling beat-em-up; three dwarves (Taconic with a baseball bat, Corthag with a shotgun, Greg with bowling balls) switch on the fly, with hit points shared by a dwarf roster; enemies: punks, cops, orcs, taxis; props: bins, crates, spikes; 15 stages plus map, intro, logo, secret; sprite packs A_*.BIN (actors), B_*.BIN (props), VDP2 scroll maps, PCM sound.
